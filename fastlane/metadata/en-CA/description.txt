Flavour Atlas is a pairing guide built for cooks. Look up an ingredient and see what it goes with, then stack ingredients into a combination and see how well they work together.

EXPLORE
• 188 ingredients: vegetables, mushrooms, herbs, fruit, spices, alliums, grains and legumes, nuts and seeds, dairy and egg, pantry staples and plant proteins like tofu, tempeh, seitan and jackfruit
• Every match is marked as a classic pairing or one that also works
• Go further: find ingredients one step away from your starting point, for combinations that are less obvious but still hold together
• Kitchen notes on technique for key pairings
• Seasonal filter, plus Canadian produce like fiddleheads, ramps, haskap, saskatoon berries and sea buckthorn

COMBO BUILDER
• Put ingredients on a ticket and get a harmony score for every pair
• The balance check covers sweet, acid, salt, umami, bitter, fat, heat, freshness and crunch, and suggests what to add for anything missing
• See what pairs with everything on the ticket, or tap Add best match

SPARK
• Random combinations of three to five ingredients, each with a technique and a dish format to aim for
• Classic, Balanced or Wild modes, an in-season filter, and an option to start from a hero ingredient
• Daily spark: get a new in-season combination as a notification each morning

MAKE IT YOURS
• Add your own ingredients, such as a local farm's variety or a house ferment, with their tastes, seasons and pairings
• Record your own pairings and notes on any ingredient
• Save combos and share them with your team
• Back up your book as text and import it on another phone

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone and the app works without a connection.
