Flavour Atlas is a self-contained native app: all content (188 ingredients, 1,400+ pairings) ships in the app, and it works fully offline with no account or server. Native features include local notifications (Daily spark, set in My book), the iOS share sheet for combos and backups, haptic feedback, and on-device storage for user-created ingredients, pairings, notes and saved combos. To test it: open Spark and tap Spark another; open any ingredient and tap Add a pairing; in My book, turn on Daily spark.
