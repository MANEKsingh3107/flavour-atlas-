Flavour Atlas Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

Flavour Atlas is a flavour-pairing guide for cooks. It is built to work without collecting anything about you.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not send any data to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

Ingredients, pairings and notes you add, your saved combinations and your settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. If you use Share backup, the text goes only where you choose to send it.

Notifications

If you turn on Daily spark, the app schedules local notifications on your device. They are created on the phone itself and involve no server.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
